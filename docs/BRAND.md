# Brand

## Fonts

From Google Fonts:

- **Display (page titles, big numbers, stamps and section labels)**: Unbounded
- **Body**: Instrument Sans
- **Accent (a tagline or a quote)**: Instrument Serif
- **Code, numbers and identifiers**: JetBrains Mono

## Colors

- **Primary**: Sea Green `#06D6A0`, Midnight text
- **Secondary**: Marigold `#FFC145`, Midnight text
- **Tertiary**: Coral `#F78C6B`, Midnight text
- **Information**: Cerulean `#118AB2`, white text
- **Highlight or alert**: Raspberry `#EF476F`, white text
- **Ink on light backgrounds**: Midnight `#073B4C`
- **Ink on dark backgrounds**: Sea Mist `#CDD8DB`

Use Sea Green, Marigold and Coral when three things sit side by side, such as a claim's states or chart series. Add Cerulean fourth and Raspberry fifth.

## Surfaces

- **Light mode**: page background `#F0F4F5`, white panels and cards
- **Dark mode**: page background `#04232E`, black panels and cards

Sea Green and Marigold are too bright for small text, so darken them for headings and body text on light backgrounds and lighten them on dark ones.

## The pop-art look

The dashboard is drawn like a comic panel, in flat brand colors.

- **Outlines**: panels, tiles, buttons and chips have a 3px outline all the way round, Midnight on light and Sea Mist on dark.
- **Hard shadows**: a solid offset shadow in the outline color, never a soft blur.
- **Halftone dots**: dot patterns fade in from the corners of the page and of each count tile.
- **Stickers**: section labels and badges are pills in capitals. Count tiles and the tagline sit a degree or two off level.
- **Speech bubbles**: the customer's story and the agent's report are speech bubbles.
- **The stamp**: a decision lands as a rubber stamp.
- **The agent**: an alien in sunglasses, which bobs while it works.
- **Motion**: short and springy. Page changes open out of a circle and a claim's id flies from the queue to its page. Everything holds still when the reader asks for reduced motion.

## Avoid

- Borders on one edge of a panel, and thin lines across the top of the page
- Heavy use of gradient fills. Fill with flat color; dots and stripes are patterns, and the one sweep of color is the outline of a run in progress
- Soft drop shadows

## Tone

Plain business English in US spelling. Short labels, no jargon and no exclamation marks. Money is in US dollars, such as $1,250.00.
